A Little Wish

I hope your pain eases soon,
your smile returns gently,
and tomorrow feels kinder.


May the little things
bring you unexpected happiness,
and may your heart find
a little more peace each day.


Until then,
take things slowly,
rest well,
and be gentle with yourself.
